# Artigo 10.º - Mais-valias

Relevant HPP reinvestment rules from the current official text:

- Gains from the sale of real rights over immovable property are capital gains.
- Gains from selling a property used as the taxpayer's or household's own permanent home are excluded from taxation if the statutory reinvestment conditions are met.
- The reinvestment base is the value of realization minus repayment of any loan originally used to acquire the sold property.
- The reinvestment may be in:
  - acquisition of ownership of another property,
  - land for construction and/or respective construction,
  - enlargement or improvement of another property,
  - in all cases exclusively for the same purpose of own permanent home.
- The replacement property may be in Portugal or in another EU/EEA state with tax information exchange.
- Timing rule: reinvestment must occur between 24 months before and 36 months after the sale date.
- The taxpayer must declare the intention to reinvest in the tax return for the year of sale, even if reinvestment is only partial.
- The sold property must have been the taxpayer's or household's own permanent home, proven through tax domicile, in the 12 months before the sale, unless exceptional circumstances apply.
- There is no benefit if:
  - on acquisition of another property, the acquirer does not assign it to own permanent home within 12 months after reinvestment,
  - in the other cases, registration/matrix requirements and later assignment deadlines are missed,
  - the taxpayer or household does not fix tax domicile in the replacement property,
  - specific anti-abuse/public-support cases in article 10(6)(d) apply.
- If reinvestment is only partial, the exclusion only applies proportionally to the reinvested part.
