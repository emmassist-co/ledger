# Artigo 43.º - Mais-valias

Relevant rule for real-estate gains:

- The taxable capital-gains income is the annual net balance of gains and losses.
- For resident taxpayers, the positive or negative balance for real-estate transfers under article 10(1)(a) is, in the ordinary case, considered only in 50% of its amount.
- There is an exception where 100% is considered in the specific public-support situation described in article 43(2)(a).
