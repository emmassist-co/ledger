# HPP reinvestment note

Assumption: this note concerns a resident individual selling a property that qualifies as habitação própria e permanente under CIRS article 10.

Current operative reading from the indexed slice:

- On the sale of a personal home, the gain is ordinarily a category G real-estate capital gain under CIRS article 10.
- In the ordinary resident case, only 50% of the net gain is considered for IRS under article 43.
- That gain can be excluded from taxation, in whole or in part, if the legal reinvestment regime for own permanent home is satisfied.
- The amount that matters for reinvestment is not the gross sale price. It is the value of realization after deducting repayment of any mortgage/loan originally taken to acquire the sold home.
- Full exclusion requires full qualifying reinvestment of that net realization amount. Partial reinvestment leads only to proportional exclusion.
- The reinvestment window is broad but strict: from 24 months before the sale to 36 months after the sale.
- The taxpayer must state the intention to reinvest in the return for the year of sale.
- The replacement property must also become the taxpayer's or household's own permanent home, with the timing conditions in article 10(6).
- The sold property must itself have been the taxpayer's or household's own permanent home, normally evidenced by tax domicile in the 12 months before sale, subject to the exceptional-circumstances rule in article 10(23).

This note is sufficient for scoped archive answers about the general HPP reinvestment rule, but it is not yet a full article-by-article personal-tax vertical for every edge case.
